Every Memory Has A Story

A journey through every conversation, dream, and moment we shared

Chapter 1

The Beginning

Himanshi,

Almost three years ago, we started talking.

In just a few days I told you how I felt.

Looking back, I understand that I rushed my feelings.

You were upset, and our conversations stopped.

Today I realise I should have been more patient.

If that decision made you uncomfortable, I'm truly sorry.

Chapter 2

Finding Our Way Again

Nearly two years later, after your birthday, I gathered the courage to message you again.

I didn't know whether you'd reply.

But you did.

And slowly...

our conversations returned.

We talked about work, dreams, life and the future.

It felt like reconnecting with someone I had genuinely missed.

Chapter 3

The Mountain Dream

We imagined going on a mountain trip together.

Talking for hours under the night sky.

Watching the moon.

Sharing silence that didn't need words.

You told me you were scared of insects.

I remember telling you I'd stay awake just to make sure you could sleep peacefully.

That memory still makes me smile.

Chapter 4

Cars

We talked about cars more than most people probably should.

I remember your favourite Porsche Panamera.

Even today...

I still remember.

Chapter 5

The Camera

I once told you I wasn't good at taking pictures.

You simply said,

"Don't worry, I'll teach you."

It was such a small sentence.

Yet somehow...

it stayed with me.

Chapter 6

The First Meeting We Imagined

We even imagined our first meeting.

You told me to come just as I am.

No special clothes.

No pretending.

Just be myself.

That meant more to me than you probably realised.

Chapter 7

Dubai

We planned a Dubai trip.

You wanted to try skydiving.

I kept saying I was scared of heights.

You patiently convinced me.

In the end...

I agreed because doing it with you somehow felt less frightening.

Chapter 8

Disneyland

We also imagined Disneyland.

Roller coasters terrified me.

Yet somehow...

you convinced me again.

I realised you had a way of making difficult things feel possible.

Chapter 9

My Biggest Mistake

There was one conversation I wish I had handled differently.

I asked something without thinking carefully about how it sounded.

Looking back,

I understand why it could have made you uncomfortable.

I never wanted to disrespect you.

If I did,

I'm deeply sorry.

Chapter 10

Navratri

When Navratri came,

I told you I couldn't come.

Maybe that disappointed you.

Maybe it didn't.

I honestly don't know.

But today I wish I had communicated better instead of making assumptions.

Chapter 11

The Day I Yelled

Out of every memory we share...

this is the one I wish I could erase.

I became frustrated.

I raised my voice.

You didn't deserve that.

There is no excuse.

Only an apology.

I'm truly sorry.
